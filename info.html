What the fork is this website

This is a personal practice tracker for speedruns. Enter your best times for each level or split, see your rank, and track your progress.

Ranks

Each level has 19 thresholds, from fastest to slowest:

The rank thresholds are based on the current WR for each IL and made-up F rank times.

Distribution Curve

Each rank threshold is computed as:

threshold(i) = WR + (F − WR) × (i / 18)1.35

where i is the rank index (0 = WR, 18 = F). The exponent controls how the 19 thresholds are spread between WR and F:

  • = 1.0 (linear) — equal gap between every consecutive rank.
  • > 1.0 (used here: 1.35) — thresholds cluster near WR; gaps grow toward F. Improving from F to D is easier than from S to WR.
